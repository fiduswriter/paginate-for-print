Liberation Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
============================================

Bundled as *metric-compatible* fallbacks for the PDF emitter: when a
document requests a family that has no matching `@font-face` rule (most
commonly "Times New Roman", "Arial" or "Courier New"), the emitter embeds
the Liberation family with identical advance widths so word positions
measured in the browser line up in the emitted PDF.

Mapping (see src/pdf/pdf-emitter.ts, METRIC_COMPATIBLE_FAMILIES):
- Times New Roman / Times / Liberation Serif -> LiberationSerif-*.ttf
- Arial / Helvetica / Liberation Sans        -> LiberationSans-*.ttf
- Courier New / Courier / Liberation Mono    -> LiberationMono-*.ttf
